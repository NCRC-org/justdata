How it works

From question to citation in three steps

  1. 1

    Choose a geography

    Pick a county or market and, where the tool supports it, a lender.

  2. 2

    Run the analysis

    Each tool runs its analysis on the federal records for the place and years you choose.

  3. 3

    Export and cite

    Download Excel tables, and in most tools a PDF report with its sources and methodology.
